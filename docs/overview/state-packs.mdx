A **State Pack** is an opinionated wedge built on top of Statis’s primitives. It provides a ready-to-use materialized state schema for specific domains.

## The Account State Pack

Provides a ready-to-use materialized state schema for customer-ops teams coordinating across Support, Billing, and CSM agents.

### Entities Tracked
- `account`
- `ticket`
- `billing_invoice`
- `csm_health`

### Reducers

The pack inherently knows how to consume `ticket.resolved` and update the `account.open_tickets_count` across domains. This prevents your AI agents from needing to know the complex side-effects of their actions.
