## What SCIM gives you

With SCIM 2.0 provisioning connected to your IdP (Okta, Entra ID, etc.):

- **Auto-provision** users when they join your organization in the IdP
- **Auto-deprovision** users and revoke access when they leave
- **Group sync** — IdP groups map to Statis RBAC roles
- **Just-in-time (JIT) access** for contractor and vendor accounts

## Supported IdPs

- Okta (SCIM 2.0)
- Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD)
- Google Workspace (via SCIM bridge)
- OneLogin
- Ping Identity
