## What VPC deployment gives you

- All Statis infrastructure runs in your cloud account (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- No data leaves your network — receipts, proposals, and policies stay on-prem
- You control the database, the encryption keys, and the backup policy
- Statis provides managed updates via Helm chart

## Architecture in VPC mode

```
Your VPC
  ├── statis-api       (FastAPI, your container registry)
  ├── statis-worker    (execution worker, your container registry)
  ├── statis-postgres  (your RDS / Cloud SQL instance)
  └── statis-console   (Next.js, your CDN or internal network)

Your agents call statis-api at your private endpoint.
Approval pages served from your domain.
```

## Setup

VPC deployment is configured during Enterprise onboarding:

1. We provide Helm charts and Terraform modules for your cloud provider
2. You provide cloud account credentials and target region
3. We deploy, validate, and hand off operational runbooks
